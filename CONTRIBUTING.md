# Contributing

Contributions should improve the clarity, consistency, applicability, or verifiability of DUSRM.

## Before proposing a concept
1. Search the repository terminology and prior-art notes.
2. Identify related established concepts and standards.
3. Explain the problem the proposal solves.
4. State whether the proposal is descriptive, informative, or intended to become normative.
5. Include examples from more than one domain where possible.

## For semantic changes
Include:
- Motivation and scope
- Proposed definition or relationship
- Alternatives considered
- Prior-art comparison
- Compatibility impact
- Validation approach
- Open questions and assumptions

## Quality expectations
Avoid product-specific language in foundational definitions. Do not claim originality without research. Diagrams must have accompanying textual definitions. Examples are illustrative and do not establish normative requirements.
