## 3. Semantic validator additions

The 0.9 semantic validator now includes conservative endpoint checks for selected relationship types:
- decomposition and containment;
- membership and dependency;
- exposure and requirements;
- interaction with external context;
- allocation, observation and control.

Relationship types without a reviewed core participant signature produce a warning rather than an invented constraint.

The 12-test suite covers allowed environment interaction, rejected control endpoints, and warnings for relationship types whose signatures remain unspecified, as well as the earlier identifier, reference, transition, evidence, and profile checks.

## 4. What the results establish

- The current structural schema checks continue to pass.
- Selected semantic checks behave as expected for the test cases.
- The distributed arbitration model preserves at-most-one authoritative owner per task under its single-coordinator abstraction, even when local claims conflict during a modeled partition.
- The FireBot logic model preserves emergency-stop dominance, manual override precedence, and actuator/source consistency.
- The ANANTA abstraction preserves single-writer ownership, WAL-before-commit, and commit-before-publication across the modeled crash/recovery transitions.

## 5. What the results do not establish

- No proof of consensus, Byzantine fault tolerance, leader election, or distributed liveness.
- No physical FireBot emergency-stop design validation, hardware verification, braking analysis, or safety certification.
- No proof of filesystem/device durability, torn-write behavior, checksums, checkpoint correctness, general ACID semantics, or MVCC in ANANTA.
- No proof that current FireBot or ANANTA source code implements the model.
- No full DUSRM conformance or validation of the universality/completeness of the eight-layer model.
- No profile compatibility approval; registry entries remain `not-assessed`.
- No automatic translation from DUSRM JSON or prose into TLA+.

## 6. Reproducibility

The workflow:
1. validates the JSON Schema fixtures;
2. runs `python -m unittest discover -s tests -v`;
3. runs positive semantic CLI examples;
4. checks expected semantic rejection of duplicate-ID and dangling-reference examples;
5. downloads TLA+ tools v1.7.4;
6. invokes TLC against each model's dedicated configuration.
