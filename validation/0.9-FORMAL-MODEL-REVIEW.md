# DUSRM 0.9 — Formal Model Review Checklist

**Status:** Internal review checklist; non-normative

## Distributed task arbitration

- [ ] Confirm local claims are not treated as authoritative assignments.
- [ ] Confirm assignment acceptance requires coordinator availability and participant connectivity.
- [ ] Check that at most one authoritative owner exists per task.
- [ ] Record that consensus and Byzantine failures are not modeled.

## FireBot emergency stop

- [ ] Confirm emergency stop disables modeled actuation.
- [ ] Confirm autonomous commands cannot be accepted while emergency stop is active.
- [ ] Confirm manual override prevents autonomous command acceptance.
- [ ] Confirm reset cannot itself enable an actuator.
- [ ] Record missing hardware and electrical fault assumptions.
- [ ] Do not treat the model as a safety case or certification.

## ANANTA WAL recovery

- [ ] Confirm one writer at most.
- [ ] Confirm commit durability requires WAL durability.
- [ ] Confirm publication requires a durable commit.
- [ ] Confirm pre-commit crash recovery discards the modeled uncommitted operation.
- [ ] Confirm post-commit/pre-publication crash recovery permits redo.
- [ ] Compare assumptions with current ANANTA WAL and transaction specifications.
- [ ] Do not infer filesystem-level durability or general ACID guarantees.

## Review result

For each item, record: pass / fail / not applicable / not assessed, supporting trace or TLC output, reviewer, tool version, and model revision.
