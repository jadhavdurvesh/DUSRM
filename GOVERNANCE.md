# Governance

## Status
DUSRM is an independent research project. Until a stable release process is adopted, the maintainers may publish explicitly marked drafts. Drafts must not be described as approved standards.

## Decision principles
- Prefer evidence, clarity, and technical consistency over naming or breadth.
- Record substantial decisions and their rationale.
- Seek review for changes to core terminology, ontology, model structure, or conformance requirements.
- Separate editorial corrections from semantic changes.
- Mark unresolved questions and competing proposals rather than hiding disagreement.

## Versioning
- Draft versions may use a 0.x series.
- A stable 1.0 specification requires a reviewed ontology, defined semantics, documented scope, validation evidence, and a conformance approach.
- Breaking semantic changes require explicit migration notes.

## Independence
DUSRM is not defined by any implementation repository. DMJ projects may provide case studies but do not automatically determine the model's normative content.
